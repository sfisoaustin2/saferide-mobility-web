Company Overview
SafeRide Mobility (Pty) Ltd is a specialized safety platform built from the ground up for independent e-hailing drivers (Bolt, Uber, and Maxim) operating across Mbombela. We provide dedicated tactical safety infrastructure tailored to vulnerable drivers on the road.
Mission & Vision
Mission: To safeguard independent drivers against roadside threats through rapid armed response integration and digital accountability.

Vision: A fear-free transport ecosystem across Mpumalanga where every driver returns home safely.
Core Values & Radical Trust
* Radical Transparency: Clear, open membership structures with zero hidden fees.
* Trust & Accountability: Verified passenger and driver profiles ensuring complete mutual safety.
* Community Resilience: Standing united to uplift local drivers against vulnerability on the road.
