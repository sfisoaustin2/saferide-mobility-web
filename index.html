Company Overview & Mission
SafeRide Mobility (Pty) Ltd is dedicated to elite tactical armed response and community safety for independent e-hailing drivers and passengers operating across Mbombela, Mpumalanga. We empower vulnerable individual drivers on Bolt, Uber, and Maxim platforms with real-time panic dispatch networks and accountability tools.
🌟 Our Core Values & Principles
🛡️ 1. Safety Above All: Prioritizing human life and physical well-being through direct PSIRA-accredited armed security coordination.
🤝 2. Independent Driver Advocacy: Protecting solo e-hailing operators on Uber, Bolt, and Maxim who lack traditional fleet security backup.
⚡ 3. Technical Excellence: Deploying real-time Firebase cloud databases, Leaflet satellite tracking, offline SMS panic backups, and anti-flooding controls.
👁️ 4. Transparency & Accountability: Maintaining permanent audit archives of all resolved incidents, responder badges, and driver trip logs.
