SafeRide Legal & Compliance

SafeRide Mobility (Pty) Ltd — Mbombela, South Africa

1. Terms and Conditions of Service

By accessing or utilizing the SafeRide Mobility platform, driver application, or control room dispatch network, you agree to be bound by these Terms and Conditions. SafeRide Mobility (Pty) Ltd provides an independent safety and verification utility designed for e-hailing drivers (including Bolt, Uber, and Maxim operators) operating within Mbombela and surrounding regions.

2. Driver Responsibilities & Conduct

  • Drivers must maintain valid identification, vehicle documentation, and correct profile info within the app.
  • The SOS emergency panic button must only be activated in genuine safety threats or emergencies requiring PSIRA private security or emergency intervention.
  • Misuse of the emergency dispatch system or false alerts may result in immediate suspension from the SafeRide network and potential liability for unnecessary deployment costs.

3. PSIRA Armed Response & Third-Party Dispatches

SafeRide Mobility facilitates direct communication lines and webhook/WhatsApp payloads to contracted private security service providers (PSIRA registered). While SafeRide endeavors to ensure rapid transmission of live GPS coordinates during emergencies, response times depend on third-party security providers, network connectivity, and regional conditions.

4. Privacy & Data Protection (POPIA Compliance)

In accordance with the Protection of Personal Information Act (POPIA):

  • Trip logs, driver profiles, and passenger verification records are stored locally on your device's secure browser storage (`localStorage`) to preserve privacy.
  • Live GPS coordinates and unit identifiers are transmitted strictly during active emergency dispatches or control room monitoring to facilitate security responses.
  • We do not sell, trade, or transfer personal driver or passenger data to unauthorized third parties.

5. Limitation of Liability

SafeRide Mobility (Pty) Ltd acts as an auxiliary safety net and communication conduit. The company shall not be held directly liable for physical injuries, criminal acts, vehicle damage, or losses incurred while operating e-hailing services. Drivers operate independently at their own discretion and risk.

6. Governing Law

These terms are governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of the Republic of South Africa, and any disputes shall be subject to the exclusive jurisdiction of the South African courts.
